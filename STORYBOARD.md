# Storyboard: When a crash turns deadly

**Audience:** local road-safety decision-makers (prefectures, départements, road-safety observatories).
**Problem:** most injury crashes are not fatal, so prevention pays off most where crashes *turn deadly*.
**Main question:** where, when and for whom does a road crash in France turn deadly (2019 to 2024)?
**Method:** six beliefs stated as hypotheses *before* the analysis, then each one confirmed, debunked or
nuanced with the data. Core metric: lethality = people killed per 100 victims.

| # | Story beat | What the user sees | Interaction | Key message |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | Title and lede: 327,628 accidents, 19,877 killed, "1 victim in 22" | None | Most victims survive, so the question is why some don't |
| 2 | **Context: KPIs** | Accidents, victims, killed, lethality | All sidebar filters | Lethality lets us compare fairly |
| 3 | **Context: COVID** | Monthly crashes (bars) and lethality (line), lockdown bands | Hover, filters | −74% crashes in April 2020, but lethality 5.30 vs 4.41: fewer crashes, not less danger |
| 4 | **Analysis: Road type** (lead finding) | Share of crashes vs share of deaths by road type | Hover, area filter | Debunked: departmental roads = 37.5% of crashes but 58.1% of deaths, motorways among the safest |
| 5 | **Analysis: Territories** | Map (lethality, deaths or fatal crash points) and ranking | Map toggle, zoom, hover | Confirmed: counts and lethality draw opposite maps, Paris 0.67 vs Creuse 12.3 |
| 6 | **Analysis: Time of day** | Hour × weekday heatmap and lighting bars | Hover, filters | Nuanced: most deaths by day, but night crashes 1.4× deadlier, unlit roads 9.65 |
| 7 | **Analysis: Age** | Share of victims vs deaths by age, lethality by age | Mode-of-travel filter | Confirmed: young people get hurt, older people die (75+: 3.7× lethality) |
| 8 | **Analysis: Holidays** | Deaths per day by type of day vs an ordinary day | Year filter | Mostly debunked, French National Day and 1 January stand out (small samples) |
| 9 | **Key insights** | Four finding cards | None | Country roads, night risk, age, COVID |
| 10 | **Implications** | Five concrete leads | None | Departmental roads, hotspot inspection, night visibility, older users, emergency coverage |
| 11 | **Data quality and limits** | Validation checks, missingness, method, limitations, sources | Tabs | Transparent about under-reporting, no exposure data, correlation vs causation |

**Limitations carried through the story:** police-recorded crashes only (non-fatal crashes are
under-reported), no traffic or population denominators, associations rather than causes, small samples for
festive days.
